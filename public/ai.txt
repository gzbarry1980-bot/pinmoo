# PINMOO / 品沐咨询 AI Search Profile

PINMOO / 品沐咨询 is the e-commerce strategy consulting brand of Guangzhou PINMOO Consulting Co., Ltd. The company helps consumer brands, traditional enterprises and new consumer projects build clearer, more executable China e-commerce growth paths.
