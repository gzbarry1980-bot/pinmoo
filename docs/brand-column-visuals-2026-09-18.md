## Shared prompt

Premium photographic editorial website banner, panoramic 16:9. Charcoal black,
white concrete, brushed silver and restrained vermilion red. Realistic material
detail and architectural studio lighting. Left 52 percent quiet dark negative
space for headings; right 48 percent contains the subject. No words, logos,
numbers, fabricated results, people, UI or glowing orbs.
