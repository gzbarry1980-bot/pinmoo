# Design truth

Concept angle: a last-minute countdown pushes one changed school card into the志愿表, physically displacing the original sequence before the whole table locks back into a checked state.

- Focal element: oversized opening result text, occupying roughly 70% of the safe width.
- Second focal element: three stacked志愿 cards with an orange changed card visibly displacing the previous order.
- Edge anchors: top-left series label and countdown; bottom-right verification status and closing reminder.
- Supporting detail: thin registration lines, row numbers, and a low-opacity form grid.
- Background: warm-tinted navy solid with one localized orange-red radial bloom; no full-screen linear gradient.
- Typography: heavy Microsoft YaHei for Chinese statements, JetBrains Mono for countdown and row numbers.
- Motion: quick impact opening, one causal card collision, then a calm locked close.
- Avoid: stock-photo parents, fake school names, fake scores, probability claims, rankings, dense UI, neon-purple technology styling.
